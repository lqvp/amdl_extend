Those three are things you check *when something is wrong*; this one is what you came to do. The two status cards are `
`, open only when they have something to say -- the wrapper opens when it is not serving, the library opens when a root cannot be read -- so a healthy hub is one form and one table. The wrapper keeps `problem` apart on purpose. "No account is logged in" gets a *Log in* button; "did not become ready" gets *Start* and a log. They are never collapsed into one "unavailable", because they ask different things of the user and the first one tells them not to wait. The queue table is rendered server-side from a real `List` and patched in place by `EventSource` frames. `data-finished` on terminal rows is what lets the browser collapse them, with a control to bring them back -- the rows stay in the DOM, because hiding them with `display: none` keeps the SSE upsert working against the same nodes. A skipped row shows its matched paths verbatim: `loose` matching is deliberately willing to treat two same-named albums as one, and those paths are the only thing a human has to overrule it with. The empty state is a permanent element toggled by `hidden`, not something the template renders conditionally: the stream can empty a queue that arrived full, and a server-rendered comment would be the one part of the page nothing updates. */}}{{ define "content" }}
