`, open only when they have something to say -- the wrapper opens when it is not serving, the library opens when a root cannot be read -- so a healthy hub is one form and one table. The wrapper keeps `problem` apart on purpose. "No account is logged in" gets a *Log in* button; "did not become ready" gets *Start* and a log.
